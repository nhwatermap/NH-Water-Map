Before you use this map

NH Water Conditions is a free, unofficial map that gathers public data from government agencies (USGS, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, NOAA, NH DES, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, NH Fish and Game and NH GRANIT) in one place. It is a planning aid only.

Data can be wrong, late or missing. Gauges fail, ice affects sensors, and agencies revise their numbers. Weather values come from a computer model, and forecasts more than a few days out are less certain.

Not for safety decisions. Do not use this map for navigation, flood decisions or ice safety. Water temperature and weather here say nothing about whether ice is safe.

Check the rules yourself. Land shown as public or managed by an agency is not a statement that hunting, fishing or access is allowed. Boundaries are approximate. Always check current regulations, property rules, postings and seasons before you go.

You are responsible for your own safety and for following the law. Conditions on the water and in the field can change quickly.

Your saved spots stay private. Markers you save are stored only in your own browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.
